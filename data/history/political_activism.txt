
This was perhaps the most politically charged feature of student life. Students were central actors in the 1919 Revolution against British rule, joining mass strikes and demonstrations behind Saad Zaghloul and the Wafd. Throughout the 1920s and 1930s, campuses simmered with nationalist agitation: strikes, protests, boycotts, and clashes with police and British authorities recurred, peaking again in the 1935 demonstrations demanding restoration of the 1923 constitution. Engineering students, alongside law students, were known for their militancy. Anti-British and pro-independence sentiment ran deep, and political commitment was woven into a student's sense of duty to Egypt.
