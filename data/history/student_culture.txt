
Students belonged to the rising *effendiyya* — a self-conscious, modern, literate class that saw itself as the future of the nation. Newspaper-reading was a daily ritual (*Al-Ahram*, *Al-Muqattam*, the nationalist *Al-Liwa*), and political debate was constant. Football was spreading rapidly and inspired fierce loyalties; literary and debating interests flourished. Camaraderie formed around shared drawing-office ordeals, exam pressure, and provincial origins. Pride in technical modernity blended with intense nationalism, producing a generation that viewed bridges, dams, and railways as instruments of national rebirth as much as careers.
